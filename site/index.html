04 / Say yes bypass mode, still safe

nothing irreversible until you say yes

Keep bypass mode. ohmyjev only stops what can't be undone, and never adds a prompt.

Stopped

  • rm -rf, force pushes, reset --hard, dropped tables
  • curl or scp sending your keys or files out
  • Writes outside the repo, or with keys in them
  • Pages that try to give your agent orders

Not stopped

  • Deleting lines, uninstalling, removing a co-author
  • Commits, pushes, installs, builds, tests
  • Anything you asked for, or said yes to
